1. Executive Summary & Vision
StudyAbroad Vista is an authoritative discovery portal and high-intent lead generation marketplace developed for Indian students, families, and working professionals looking to study abroad. It bridges Indian aspirants with accredited international universities and verified educational consultants.

Key Project Objectives:
• Programmatic SEO Engine: 1,080+ to 1,290+ high-speed, indexed URLs covering 19 destinations and 6 academic disciplines.
• Conversion & Lead Qualification: Multi-step verified lead capture (SMS OTP via MSG91) matching students with consultants.
• AI Counsellor (RAG): 24/7 conversational assistant using Google Gemini 2.0/1.5 Flash + Supabase pgvector knowledge retrieval.
• Student Utility Suite: Cost of Living & Tuition Calculator, University Comparison Tray, and Eligibility Checker.
• Four Dedicated Portals: Public Discovery Site, Student Dashboard, B2B Buyer Portal (Razorpay wallet), and Admin CMS.

4. Essential Third-Party Integrations for Indian Market
Beyond the core platforms (Next.js, Node.js, Supabase, Sanity.io, Vercel, Gemini), the following 5 services are critical for MVP operations:

1. Razorpay (Payments & Prepaid B2B Wallet):
   Enables admission consultants to top-up their lead purchase wallet in INR via UPI, Netbanking, and Cards, with automated GST invoice generation.

2. MSG91 / Twilio (Indian Mobile SMS OTP):
   Authenticates student phone numbers at lead capture to eliminate fake inquiries before leads reach paying consultants.

3. Resend / SendGrid (Transactional Email):
   Sends branded emails for booking confirmations, lead delivery receipts, and account authentication.

4. WhatsApp Business API (High Conversion):
   Automates immediate student notifications and consultation reminders. WhatsApp open rates in India exceed 90%.

5. Google Search Console & GA4 (Free):
   Monitors crawl rate, indexing coverage, and keyword rankings across 1,080+ programmatic SEO pages.
5. System Architecture & Subsystem Design
• Unified Data Engine (Supabase):
  A single PostgreSQL database powers core entities (users, leads, buyers, wallets, universities, programs),   vector embeddings (rag_embeddings with pgvector), and authenticated file storage.

• Editorial Content Lake (Sanity.io):
  Pillar guides, blog articles, scholarship write-ups, and visa guides are managed in Sanity Studio.   When editors publish, a webhook triggers Next.js On-Demand Incremental Static Regeneration (ISR), updating pages in <500ms.

• Fast Typo-Tolerant Search (Postgres FTS + pg_trgm):
  A generated tsvector column with GIN index coupled with trigram extension provides instantaneous search with fuzzy typo-correction.

• Conversational AI Counsellor (Gemini + RAG):
  Google Gemini 2.0/1.5 Flash paired with text-embedding-004. Embeddings match via cosine similarity SQL queries in Supabase.   Gemini natively executes function tools (search_universities, calculate_costs, save_shortlist, book_counsellor_call).
